# Known limitations

This is a working private product demo. It is not ready to accept renters or publish to the public.

- The sandbox has an explicit demo role switcher. Replace it with production identity, staff provisioning and MFA; complete account recovery and abuse review.
- Password verification/reset links are shown in the UI because mail delivery is disabled.
- Providers, addresses, prices, availability, personas and reviews are fictional. Review covers submitted information only. There are no identity, authority, ownership, title, licence or property-condition checks.
- Room photos are licensed stock and labelled illustrative. Uploads accept JPEG, PNG and WebP up to 3 MB, check file signatures, provider ownership and daily volume. There is no malware scan; add scanning, dimension limits, deletion lifecycle and private media delivery before production.
- No map, commute estimate, payment, rent/deposit transfer, escrow, lease signing, university partnership, sponsored inventory or real property tour is active. Tour URLs are HTTPS and limited to Matterport or Kuula.
- EN, zh-HK and zh-CN cover key navigation and forms. Some long descriptions, operations states and explanatory text remain English.
- Automated tests cover core profiles, filters, scoring, saves, introductions, ownership, listing review, reports, roommate consent and account recovery. Browser, visual and screen-reader QA could not be completed in this environment.
- Complete qualified legal/privacy review, data retention/residency decisions, accessibility QA, production monitoring, incident handling, backups, recovery and abuse safeguards before real users join.
