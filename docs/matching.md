# Matching rules (`v1`)

Hard checks run first: listing and provider must be live and approved; monthly rent must be within budget unless the student opted into a clearly labelled margin of up to 10%; room type and occupancy must fit; lease length and known availability must overlap the requested move-in and lease dates. Missing or unconfirmed availability is displayed as unknown and earns no date points.

Eligible homes receive up to 100 normalized points across available criteria: budget 30, neighbourhood 20, move-in/lease 20, room type 15, explicitly selected amenities 10, and verified routing 5. Routing is not configured, so its weight is omitted and the score is normalized across the available criteria. Unselected amenities are neutral. Missing profile data produces “add preferences,” not a fabricated score.

Each result includes the version, score, criteria used, positive reasons, trade-offs, missing-data notes and hard-filter reasons. Scores describe preference fit; they are not a safety score, lease recommendation, availability guarantee or paid ranking.

Roommate matching requires both profiles visible, at least one shared area, move-in dates within 62 days, and budgets within a 65% ratio. Soft-fit weights are area 40%, dates 25%, budget 20% and chosen-priority overlap 15%. The feed shows when missing priorities limit confidence. Sensitive traits are not used.
