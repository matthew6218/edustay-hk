# Demo walkthrough

1. Search HKU, Kennedy Town and a monthly ceiling. Sample listings display a fictional-data badge.
2. Choose **Try the demo → Alex**. Complete or edit a housing profile; change the budget or room type to see hard filters and fit reasons.
3. Save a home, refresh, open the shortlist and inspect rent, deposit and availability details. Request an introduction and read the sharing notice.
4. Switch to **Harbour Nest**. View a lead, accept contact and reply in the thread. Switch back to Alex to see the status update.
5. As an approved provider, draft a listing, upload an owned image or choose labelled sample media, save or submit, and confirm availability.
6. As **Demo reviewer**, review provider and listing submissions, publish a sample listing, or resolve a report. Audit entries record status changes and reasons.
7. As Alex, open **Roommates**, opt in and choose up to three priorities. Switch to **Jamie** to accept a connection; messaging appears after acceptance. Pausing or blocking revokes access.

All data is fictional. Changes are shared across tabs in the same browser profile and stay on that device. Do not enter identity documents, real rental inquiries or sensitive information.
