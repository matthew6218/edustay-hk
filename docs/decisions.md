# Product decisions and launch gates

| Decision | Demo behaviour | Production owner / status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market role | Discovery, preference matching and provider introduction; EduStay is not the landlord or an escrow service. | Legal/product: review activity and licence scope. |
| Seed inventory | Stable fictional provider, student and property records; stock photos labelled illustrative. | Supply/design: secure rights and real inventory. |
| Trust review | Reviewer approves submitted fields only. Public labels disclose this limited scope. | Trust/legal: define evidence, review standards, retention and renewal. |
| Rent, deposits, fees | All prices are fictional; no checkout, custody, commission, subscription or escrow. | Finance/legal: approve contracts, fees, refunds and any regulated partner. |
| Email and verification | Registration works with hashed passwords. Verification/reset links appear in the demo because email is disabled. | Engineering: configure email, abuse controls and recovery. |
| Staff permissions | Role-scoped endpoints; initial reviewer is available only through the private demo switcher. | Security: managed provisioning and mandatory staff MFA. |
| Roommates | Separate explicit opt-in, limited preferences and mutual-consent chat. | Trust: review moderation and retention. |
| Location and commute | Curated neighbourhood labels; no travel-time estimates. | Product/data: choose licensed route source and refresh policy. |
| Languages | Core navigation and form labels are translated; long-form guidance still needs review. | Localization: complete EN, zh-HK and zh-CN before launch. |
| Hosting and privacy | Sites D1/R2 storage; locally emulated for development. | Privacy/security: approve region, processor terms, transfers, backup and deletion schedule. |
| Marketing claims | No university endorsement, live-inventory guarantee, rating aggregate, rent statistic or safety certification. | Marketing: substantiate real-world claims before publication. |
